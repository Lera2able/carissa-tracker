How it works Fill in the sections below. Your entries are stored as a draft on this device while you work. When you are done, choose “Download filled form” — the document downloads as a filled PDF on the official Carissa Primary School letterhead.
What it covers Pre-moderation of the paper before it is written, post-moderation after marking, and the item analysis grid. The State and Comments boxes are large — write full sentences.

Analyse 10% of the scripts (minimum 5). Enter the mark out of per question, then each learner’s mark per question. Totals, averages, percentages and the 7-point level calculate automatically.

Leave unused learner rows empty — they stay blank on the document.
