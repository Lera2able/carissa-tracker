3 Learner question grid

Write the question learners struggled with in the top row. The boxes are taller, grow as you type, and can be resized. Then pick up to 15 learners from any class in this grade and enter their marks for Q1–Q10. The “Mark out of” and total for each question are used in section 4.

Learners come from all classes in the grade you selected in step 1.
