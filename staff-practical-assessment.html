How the test works

The test has three exercises and counts 40 marks. You have 60 minutes in total: about 5 minutes to read this page, then 55 minutes of timed work.

ExerciseWhat you will doMarksTime
1Asset register verification: find the discrepancies and explain what you would do1515 min
2Spreadsheet: capture, sort and filter an asset list, check for duplicates and write a summary1525 min
3Scenario: a physical asset verification that goes wrong1015 min
  • Each exercise has its own countdown. When the time runs out the test moves on to the next exercise by itself, so use your time well.
  • You can finish an exercise early, but you cannot go back to it afterwards.
  • The spreadsheet is built into the page, so you do not need Microsoft Excel. It lets you type in cells, sort, filter and highlight duplicates.
  • Your answers are saved on this device as you type. If the page reloads, your clock keeps running and your answers come back.
  • When you finish, or when the last timer ends, your test is submitted automatically.
  • Work on your own and answer in your own words. Short, clear and practical answers score best.
