How to Play

This version feels more like NitroType: countdown, live opponents, nitro boost, and a final race table. It still sends your mark back to the teacher automatically when opened from LearnWorld.

The Race

  • Type the text as fast and accurately as you can.
  • Your car moves forward with every correct character.
  • AI classmates race beside you with different speeds.
  • The race ends when you finish the text or the timer runs out.

Nitro Boost

  • Accurate typing builds your nitro bar.
  • When nitro is full, your car gets a short visual boost.
  • Mistakes reduce nitro and hurt your final score.

Teacher scoring

  • WPM, accuracy, time, and summary are sent back automatically.
  • Teachers can monitor the class in LearnWorld Track Progress.
  • The built-in teacher view remains the official leaderboard.

Difficulty

  • Easy: short sentences and slower AI.
  • Medium: longer school-level sentences.
  • Hard: advanced sentences and faster AI.
  • Expert: long passages and tough opponents.
